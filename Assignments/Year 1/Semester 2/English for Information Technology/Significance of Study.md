The study aims

Our research can help young adults/adolescent understand the benefits and risks of incorporating technology into developing romantic relationships, thereby aiding them to form healthier and stronger relationships. Our research may also help individuals avoid falling into trap of catfishing and online deception especially on dating apps such as Tinder by highlighting signs and scenarios.  Moreover, a survey is conducted to investigate how technology influences young adults’ romantic relationships as well as gauging their view on catfishing or online deception. Furthermore, the survey aims to determine their opinion on the outlook of integrating technology with romantic relationships

==The study aims to determine the negative impacts of cybercrime towards Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman (UTAR) undergraduate students through a target sample of UTAR Faculty of Information and Communication Technology (FICT) undergraduate students and the respective solutions to the problems faced. The study would be beneficial to UTAR undergraduate students in terms of identifying and highlighting the existing cybercrime cases which are faced by them. As a consequence, further actions and measures could be taken to raise the awareness of the students regarding cybercrime in an effective yet efficient manner, therefore preventing UTAR undergraduate students from falling victim to similar cybercrime.==
