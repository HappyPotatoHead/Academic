Technology is a dynamic and an integral aspect of civilisation, placing an irrevocable effect in shaping how we live, work and converse with one another. Technology encompasses the utilization of scientific knowledge to develop tools, systems, and solutions that allow humanity to exploit to overcome seemingly insurmountable challenges and elevating human capabilities to heights unimaginable. With seemingly limitless potential, technology has evolved exponentially since the dawn of civilisation, giving rise to groundbreaking inventions and revolutionary theories. (Luenendonk, 2023)

Technology is an umbrella term that encompasses a wide array of fields, applications, conundrum they solve, the objective of each innovation. If a person wishes to comprehend and solve the complexities of the human body, he can refer to medical technology where cutting edge technology and knowledge is utilised to drive contemporary medical innovation. Another relevant field is the communication technology where everyone is able to contact and converse conveniently whenever -  voice and video calls, text messages - with anyone from across the globe. Additionally, browsing websites, reading blogs, and scrolling through social media are all made possible with communication technology. Furthermore, a field that most citizens are familiar with is the electronic technology. According to Leunendonk on 2023, electric-powered devices and systems used to process and transmit information fall under this category. Branching further, an innumerable number of categories of devices - laptop, phones, tablets, and communications systems - fall under this category. 

According to Stritof on 2022, the definition of romantic love differs from person to person, culture to culture, region to region; however, the general consensus is that a bonded relationship stems from a combination of mutual attraction and idealisation. People involved in a romantic relationship has to traverse through hurdles - from the "honeymoon" phase to a sense of dread and longing for excitement, and, hopefully, to accepting the partner for who they are and a desire for an ever-lasting relationship. Meeting life partners through online means is becoming more common than ever with one-third of matrimonies stems from online connection (Cacioppo, 2013, as cited in Kwok & Wescott, 2020); however, to better grasp our digital world, we continue to rely on our innate human instincts, providing such technologies our own definitions and interpretations (Kwok & Wescott, 2020). Prensky (2001, as cited in Kwok & Wescott, 2020) found that digital natives - individuals raised in a technological era - are known to be extraordinarily proficient at cyber-intimacy with researches denoting increased accordance between their digital and physical love lives compared to other groups -  adolescents exchange passwords to show commitment in a relationship according to a study (Lucero et al., 2014). 


Nevertheless, we are only beginning to unravel and understand how cyber-intimacy influences romantic relationships in spite of the irrevocable changes technology has brought upon us (Kwok & Wescott, 2020).
