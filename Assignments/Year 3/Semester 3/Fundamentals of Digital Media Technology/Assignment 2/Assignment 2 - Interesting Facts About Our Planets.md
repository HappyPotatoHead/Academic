# Content

Since the title is explicitly *Interesting **Facts** About **Our Planets***, the content has to revolve around the extraterrestrial bodies within our solar system. Having to present only **facts** removes a lot of ambiguity of the content, so most of the effort should focus on the delivery of the content.

Planets can be divided into multiple classifications, but we will focus on those that are familiar [^1]:
1. Inner Planets
	- These planets are formed within the confine of the asteroid belt (nearer to the sun)
	- These are also known as terrestrial planets (*because they have solid surfaces*)
		- This is the case because the inner planets are formed within the confines of the frost line. 
		- A unique characteristic of our solar system is that the frost line and asteroid belt overlap almost perfectly. 
	- Planets
		- Mercury
		- Venus
		- Earth 
		- Mars
3. Outer Planets
	- These planets are formed outside the confine of the asteroid belt (further from the sun)
	- Because these planets formed in areas that are simply too cold, they're usually gas giants or ice giants
	- Gas giants
		- Jupiter
		- Saturn
	- Ice Giants
		- Uranus
		- Neptune
4. Dwarf Planets
	- Ceres
	- Pluto
