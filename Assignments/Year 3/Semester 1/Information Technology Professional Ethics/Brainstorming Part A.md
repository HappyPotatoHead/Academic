(*There is also the idea that this is caused by the panic hiring during the pandemic being thrown around, but we don't talk about that*)

The company that was responsible of the Windows blackout all over the world had [laid off 500 employees as AI streamlines business](https://www.msspalert.com/news/crowdstrike-cutting-5oo-jobs-as-ai-streamlines-business)

~~Google~~ Alphabet's CEO, Sundai Pichai, said that [over 25\% of new Google code is AI generated](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2024/10/google-ceo-says-over-25-of-new-google-code-is-generated-by-ai/), which, besides the decrease in the desire to hire fresh-graduates, is a cause for concern.

## Data Privacy in Enterprise Systems

Enterprise Systems refer to systems that are used by large co-operations to handle their day-to-day business. These systems are able to function with user data. 

Data privacy refers to the legalities, policies, and frameworks meant to protect user data; it keeps these companies from misusing or abusing the user's data. For example, under these laws, companies are not allowed to sell their users' data to another third party firm. Under data privacy, it also includes what the company does to protect user data - only authorised users are allowed to access sensitive data or protecting user data from data breaches. Thus, in essence, data privacy is an umbrella term that generally refers to safeguarding user data by any means necessary. 

There are also laws and regulations in which organisations have to comply to such as the [PDPA](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/portal/content/654).  

Combine data privacy with enterprise systems, essentially, it refers to how large businesses or conglomerates handle user data.

This topic can also refer to how the data is gathered and how the company uses user data within the company. There are a lot of data breaches going around lately and a lot of user data misuse nowadays.
