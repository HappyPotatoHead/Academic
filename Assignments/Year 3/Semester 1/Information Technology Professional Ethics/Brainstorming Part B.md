# Cyberbullying or online harassment

> Cyberbully means someone uses the internet to threaten or frighten another person, often by sending them unpleasant or hurtful messages. It is similar to the conventional bullying. The purpose of both types of bullying is to hurt or embarrass the victims.
>
> Cyberbullying happens 24/7 and online through electronic devices. It is anonymous and has the potential to broadcast to a wider audience. It doesn't have to confront people face-to-face. Traditional bullying happens in person at public places, especially school. For example, physical bullying
>
> Online harassment:
> Online harassment is a type of abuse that occur through the internet. It can be any behaviour that can insult or humiliate someone at online. For example, threat or like jimmy always stalk someone or share private image, blackmail, posting embarrassing media of someone without his or her consent
