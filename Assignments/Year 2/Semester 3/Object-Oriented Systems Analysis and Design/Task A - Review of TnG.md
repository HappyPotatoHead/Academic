# Analysis

The reward System in Touch ‘n Go is the GOreward programme; a loyalty programme that allows users to earn points from the transactions and payments that they make. These collected points can then be used to redeem rewards, vouchers, or discounts on selected items. The users can earn these points whenever they make a transaction via a merchant’s QR code, DuitNow QR code issued by TnG eWallet, by showing their own QR code during payment, or by using the Touch ‘n Go eWallet Visa Card[^1]. Because DuitNow QR code can be issued by both banks and non-banks, its presence is ubiquitous, making earning GOrewards points frequent and easy[^2]. Additionally, users can also earn points by utilising TnG eWallet, or TnG eWallet Visa Card during transactions on these services: e-commerce, merchants, bills, insurance, and remittances. However, only insurance offered by TnG themselves will earn the users their points.

In this programme, for every Ringgit Malaysia spent, users accrue one GOreward point. The points are awarded based on the whole Ringgit amount spent, disregarding any cents. For instance, if a user makes a RM5.90 transaction, only 5 points will be awarded. Additionally, the points earned are automatically forfeited if they are not utilised within six months of the month they were earned[^3]. Fortunately, TnG tells the users how much points along with the date at which the points will expire, relieving the users from keeping track of it themselves.

![[point-expiration.png]]The registration process is also made simple; a user only has to perform two actions - click ‘Start Collecting Points’ under the GOrewards card and then click “Join Now” [4]. By making this process quick and easy, it incentivises users to participate in this programme.

![[GOrewards-interface.png]]

Upon entering the GOrewards section, the user is shown their current point balance clearly at the top, making it easy to see their available points. The “Redeem now” button is also placed next to the point balance, eliminating the need for unnecessary navigation by ensuring immediate access, inadvertently encouraging users to redeem their points. Each section of the GOrewards programme is also easily accessible to the user with the iconised navigation tabs – “Catalogue”, “My Rewards”, “Games”, and “Point History”, making it effortless and convenient. The users are also shown the different types of rewards offered under “Explore catalogues”.

TnG offers users a diverse range of redemption options, including gifts, lucky draw entries, gift cards, discount vouchers for various stores, e-commerce site’s – Lazada – vouchers, and travel-related rewards. TnG also lets users to purchase physical products and redeem points during transactions to get discounts. Digital vouchers and gift cards are typically delivered instantly within the app with a promo code for the user to use during transactions, while physical items are processed for delivery. Upon confirmation, the corresponding points are automatically deducted from the user’s balance.

![[item-redemption.png]] ![[my-reward.png]]

When the user finds an item, they are interested in, the amount of point needed to redeem the item is clearly displayed along with the range of valid date. Additionally, where and how to use the item will be written on the page. If the user has inadequate points, the user will not be able to redeem the item and will be shown the point balance that they currently have. Once the user redeems their selected items, they can view it under the “My Rewards” section.

In conclusion, the GOrewards programme provides a straightforward and rewarding experience for Touch ‘n Go users through its easy point accumulation and diverse redemption options. The seamless integration with the TnG eWallet also allows users to access this programme wherever and whenever.
