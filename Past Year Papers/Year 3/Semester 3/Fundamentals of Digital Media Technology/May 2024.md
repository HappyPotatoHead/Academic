Vector graphics are graphics that are represented mathematically rather than with sequences of bits. A vector graphic is made from smaller vector components. Each of these components have their own mathematical representation. This makes editing each component isolated from one another as their mathematical representation is different.    - The design should include elements and icons that are easily identifiable, allowing users to identify their purposes intrinsically

   - The design of one page should be consistent with the design of another page.

3. Clarity of information
   - The user interface should use colour scheme and typography that make information readible. For example, the colour used for text should contrast the background enough to be discernable while preventing halation.
4. Convenience of navigation
   - The user should be able to navigate to their desired location within three clicks to avoid frustrations.

The main content of the page should also be positioned in a very obvious manner.
