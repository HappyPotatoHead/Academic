2. Short-form content
    - Advantages
        1. Able to provide information as quickly as possible
            - Short-form videos forces creators to be more concise in the way they convey information.
            - This led to more creative methods of providing their content
        2. Easier reproduction
            - Generally requires fewer resources to produce or reproduce.
            - This allows creators to react, create, or reproduce trends.
    - Disadvantages
        1. Affected user's attention span
            - Studies have shown that short-form content has negative impact on user's attention span, where users can be addicted to scrolling social media.
            - The anticipation of the next content has negatively impacted users' dopamine baselines. 
            - Creates this behaviour coined "doom scrolling"
        2. Sensory overload
            - Short-form content forces creators to shove as much information as possible into a short video.
            - This causes certain videos to have too many moving elements that may overload the user.
            - The user does not retain much information in the end.
            - Short video contents also shows new videos rapidly, preventing the user from any breaks

### Question A

1. Loss of information
    - Lossless compression ensures that original data is kept after each compression and decompression cycle, while in lossy compression, data is lost after each compression-decompression cycle.
2. Compression ratio
    - Since lossless compression keeps all of the original data, the compression ratio is generally lower than lossy compression where data is lost.
3. Different file size
    - Lossless compression produces compressed files that are larger in size since it preserves the original information while lossy compression produce smaller file size as information are lost
4. Computationally slower
    - Lossless compression has to perform heavy processing to ensure that no data is loss, while lossy compression remove data that is deemed not-noticible
