### Question A

#### Question I -> this is like the standard template for benefits of multimedia.

1. It can help with *information retention* amongst students -> defacto point for education
    - Multimedia engages multiple senses amongst students, such as sight, vision, and hearing.
    - This allows the brain to create more neural connections in relation to the information that they are receiving. 
    - When auditory and visual stimuli are paired, the brain encodes the information through dual coding, making it easier to recall than if it had been presented through a single medium.

2. The students can learn the information at their own pace -> this is defacto too
    - The interactivity in multimedia technology allows the students to control the speed in which the information is presented to them.
    - For example, multimedia technology allows students to fastfoward, go backward, or pause when viewing a video.
    - It also allows students to control the content that they are watching.
    - For example, the students may be able to pick the specific module that they are interesting in to learn around, as opposed to following a strict sequential timeline.

3. Helps convey complex information easily -> this is also defacto
    - Rather than just relying on texts to convey information, which requires students to visualise the information themselves, multimedia application can reduce that burden by providing a visualisation that the student can base off of.
    - For example, describing how our solar system via text can lead to multiple intrepretations that students can get them wrong; with the help of graphics, the student has an objective visuals that shows them how the solar system actually looks like.
    - Using multimedia also creates a narrative environment, allowing students to follow the information easily.

### Question C

#### Question I

A working storyboard is a storyboard that is primarily used by the development team. 
It is not meant to be presented to external and internal stakeholders. 
It is meant to represent the vision that the project manager has. 
A working storyboard contains technical information such as fonts used, their sizes, colours, the images and audios used and their types.

For example, the storyboard used for the animation team. The storyboarding contains technical information such as audio fx, dialogue, director's notes

##### Question II

Presentation storyboard is a storyboard meant to be presented to both external and internal stakeholders.
Project managers use this storyboard to secure approval and funding.
Because it is meant to be presented, it is designed to be visually appealing and easy to follow.
Thus, the technical details are excluded, leaving only the prototype to be presented. 
However, the slides presented in presentation storyboard do not represented the final work exactly.

#### Question III

Thumbnail or rough storyboard is a storyboard that is used to capture ideas quickly. 
It is not meant to be used by the development team during the development process of the multimedia project. 
It is used during brainstorming session or when gathering clients' requirements. 
With thumbnail storyboard, misunderstanding of the requirements can be cleared up quickly, as it provides a way to ensure that both the development team and the client are sharing the same vision.

### Question B

1. Modelling
    - This step involves creating the three dimensional models of the virtual objects.
    - The developers define the joints and the bones in the three dimensional models for the subsequent step.
    - Created digitally or scanned from real-world objects.
    - Depending on the degree of movements the developers wish to incorporate into the models, the developers can add more bones and joints, creating more fluid movements.
2. Animating
    - Animating refers to the definition of the movements of the models, changes in the camera movements, and the lighting.
    - This step heavily involves inverse kinematics, the study of the movements of joints.
        - The movement of one joint connects to the movement of another naturally,
        - This is important as it reduces the amount of manual work as the developers define the movements in their models
3. Rendering
    - This step is to load the textures, colours, as well as the degree of transparency.
    - Usually, the developers will render in low quality or lower resolution settings to ensure that it is error free, as this step consumes a lot of computing power.
