	1. This ethical code revolves around being honest with their behaviour and actions as well as remaining accountable

	1. An IT professional should always be finding ways to improve themselves further
	2. For example, they should attend workshops to learn new skills

	1. IT professionals should treat each other with respect regardless of who they are
	2. For example, a product manager should not be bias or prejudice over one person than another
