## Question 2
### Question a
VLAN is safer compared to LAN. This is because VLAN reduces the risk of broadcast storm attack. Broadcast storm attack is where hackers spam packets into a network and floods the network devices until they become congested, buffered and crashed. A smaller broadcast domain reduces the number of hosts affected. 
VLAN allows user to be in a different physical location while connected to their VLAN. This is because VLAN has VLAN spanning. Different switches with the same VLAN can communicate with each other.
