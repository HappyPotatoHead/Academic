### Question B
>[!Note] If you can't think of an example
> Just draw one straight line.


Whether a lattice is distributive lattice can be identified using the formula
$a \; \lor \; (b \land c) \equiv \; (a\;\lor \; b)\land(a \; \lor \; c)$ 
$\lor$ is used to find the Least Upper Bound and $\land$ is used to find Greatest Lower Bound
Avoid picking the least and greatest as the equation will always be true if those two are chosen
![[distributive_lattice]]
**Take straight line cuz I'm lazy**
$a \; \lor \; (b \land c) \equiv \; (a\;\lor \; b)\land(a \; \lor \; c)$ 
**LHS**
$$\begin{align}
a \; \lor \; (b \land c) \\
a \; \lor \; c \\
a
\end{align}
$$
**RHS**
$$\begin{align}
(a \; \lor \; b) \land \; (a \land c) \\
a \; \lor \; c \\
a
\end{align}
$$
**LHS = RHS**
