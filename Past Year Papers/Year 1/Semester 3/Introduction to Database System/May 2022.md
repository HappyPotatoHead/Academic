## Question 5
### Question A
#### Question I
**Simple attribute**
- attribute that cannot be divided further into components with an independent existence
- Age attribute of a student
**Composite attribute**
- Attributes that can be divided into more components, each with independent existence
- Home address of a person can be divided into street, state, and zip
#### Question II
**Single-value attribute**
- Attribute that can hold a single value in each occurrence in an entity
- employee ID
**Multi-valued attribute**
- Attribute that can hold multiple values in each occurrence in an entity
- Colour of a car
