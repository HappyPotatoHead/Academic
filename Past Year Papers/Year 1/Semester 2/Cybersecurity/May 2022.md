#### Question i
File encryption serves to preserve confidentiality whereas digital signature serves to provide integrity. File encryption uses the key pairs to change the data or file sent into unreadable format whereas digital signatures uses the key pair to determine if the data has been corrupted by comparing the hash values. File encryption aims to preserve integrity by preventing users from modifying the content of the data whereas digital signature provides integrity by detecting if the data has been corrupted. File encryption schemes can be symmetric or asymmetric whereas digital signature only uses public and private key pairs. File encryption does not provide non-repudiation whereas digital signature provides non-repudiation. This means in digital signatures, parties involved cannot deny their involvement or any changes made by them

1. Denial of Service attacks
	1. The attacks aim to disrupt a network or a server by sending in a flood of commands or traffic, which, in return, will deny legitimate users from accessing services. 
	2. The attack can be a form of diversion for a more lethal attack to launch, or it's an attempt to shutdown a server
	3. The IDS system detects DoS attacks by detecting any unusual surge in traffic activity which may indicate a DoS attack
2. Man-in-the-middle attacks
	1. An attacker intercepts the data or information that is being transmitted from one point to another. The attacker will use the information intercepted for their own benefit - accessing the victim's personal account or financial information.
	2. The IDS detect this by monitoring the channel for unusual changes in traffic, unprecedented redirection, or discrepancy from the usual traffic, and raises an alarm. 
3. Malware
	1. Malware comprises viruses, worms, Trojan Horse, and ransomware. The malware aims to damage the system and steal important information from the victim.
	2. The IDS detects this by matching the pattern of the malware with the existing patterns in the system's database.

### Question C
#### Transfer
The process of transferring risk to another asset, process, or organisation. If lacking, an organisation can reach out to other organisation or firms that specialises in security management and administration. The organisation can then transfer the risk associated with managing complex system to those who specialise in them
#### Terminate
The company or organisation stop or does not implement the service that would cause uncontrollable vulnerabilities or risks. 
#### Acceptance
Accepting the outcome of the exploitation. This happens usually when the asset itself is not that important
#### Mitigation
The process of attenuating the outcome of the exploitation. The plans that are put in place are disaster recovery plan, incident response plan, and business continuity plan
#### Defence
The most popular option. It is the process of implementing security procedures and countermeasures to defend against the attack such as isolating the affected resources from the rest
