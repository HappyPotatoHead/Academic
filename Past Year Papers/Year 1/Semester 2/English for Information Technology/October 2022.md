## What are the methods universities can use to take action on data protection and cybersecurity?
Universities can organise awareness campaigns. Universities can also invest in simple safeguards. For instance, set up multifactor authentication for those who use their IT infrastructure. Furthermore, university should routinely use virtual private networks when accessing sensitive data from outside the institution. Moreover, in the long term, students in academia should be encouraged to take responsibility for cybersecurity as they will be the ones dealing with threats in the future. Furthermore, universities can organise an ethical hacking competition to encourage students to think about cybersecurity issues.
