		- Leadership
		- Technical
			- Has to speak to vendor 
		- Conflict management
		- Customer relationship
2. **Project**
	- Planned undertaking of related activities to reach an objective that has a beginning and an end.
3. **Deliverable**
	- An end product of an SDLC phase
	- Develop a software of quality
4. **Project management:**
	- A controlled process of **initiating**, **planning**, **executing**, and **closing down** a project

### Processes
**Definition**
- **SSR**
	- SSR is a form requesting development or maintenance of an information system. It includes the contact person, a problem statement, and a service request statement
- **Project charter**
	- Project charter – a short (typically one page), high-level document prepared for both internal and external stakeholders
		- **Internal**
			1. Team member
			2. Manager
			3. Higher manager
		- **External**
			1. People outside of the company
			2. Customer
			3. People funding the project
1. **Initiation team**
	- Organising an initial core of project team members to assist in accomplishing project initiation
		- *The people that you want to add into the group*
		- *These people will have tasks to initiate the project*
			- Talk to people
			- Learn necessary skills
			- Technology needed
			- Sufficient funds
2. **Relationship with the customer**
	- Manager maintaining good relationship with the customer to identify their interests 
3. **Project initiation plan**
	- Define activities required to organise team while working to define the goals and scopes of the project
		- Agendas for meetings 
		- Setting deadlines for each tasks
			- Is the system popular
			- What is the potential market for the system
	- Creation of System Service Request (*for interest customer from the second phase*)
		- Customer can submit document/requests to the developer to implement their requests. 
4. **Management procedures**
	- Develop team communication, job assignment, and reporting procedures, project funding (**S**tandard **O**perating **P**rocedures)
		- how often should they report their progress to the manager
		- How the team would communicate among themselves
		- How the manager will report to the higher managements
			- Time 
			- Money 
			- Progress
	- The report is brief and surface level since the project has yet to begin. 
		- It takes about how many customers are interested 
		- The manpower needed
		- The technology needed
		- The market 
5. **Project management environment**
	- collect and organise tools that will be used to manage the project and to construct the project workbook (diagrams, charts, system description)
	- Document the team member's work
	- The document records the details about the system/project
		- The roles of each member
		- The finding of each member
		- How the manager reports
		- Who funds the project
		- Target market of the project
		- **JUST DETAILS OF THE PROJECT**
6. **Project Workbook**
	- [?] an online or hard-copy repository for all project correspondence, inputs, outputs, deliverables, procedures that are used. To review project status, for project audits, orientation of new members and performing post project reviews. 
		- It's a *textbook* of the project
		- This will be used for project audit later
			- Checks if the funds given had been utilised properly by the manager
				- Has it been pocketed
			- Has the project provide profit as promised
			- Are customer requests fulfilled
	- Allows new member to comprehend the project *Documentation*
7. **Develop project charter**
	- project title, date of authorization, project manager name & contact info, customer name, start & completion date, objectives, key assumptions, stakeholders role and **signature of key stakeholders.**
	- Presenting this during a meeting with the internal and external stakeholders
		- Authorise and officially begin the project
	- **TO GET APPROVAL FROM THE STAKEHOLDERS**
		- will make money anot
	- **CUSTOMER AGREES THAT THEY WANT SUCH A SYSTEM**
