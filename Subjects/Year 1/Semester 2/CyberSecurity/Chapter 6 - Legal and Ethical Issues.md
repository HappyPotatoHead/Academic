# Computers and Cyber crime
## Computer as Targets
- Stealing information from a computer system
- Accessing a computer system without authorisation or payment
## Computer as Storage Device
- Using computer to store stolen information
	- Password
	- Token
	- Account number
	- Financial information
- Using computer to store illegal files
	- Pornographic materials
	- CP
## Computer as Communication device
- Committing traditional crimes online
	- Selling drugs
	- Selling pornographic materials online
# Ethics
## Roles of Computer
1. Repositories and processor of information
2. Producer of new forms and types of asset
3. Instruments of acts
## IT code of conduct
- A written guideline that helps people determine which actions or activities are considered ethical
### Unethical behaviour
1. **I**gnorance
	1. Ignorance of policies and procedures
	2. Conduct training, awareness program, and reminders
2. **A**ccident
	1. Some employees may cause damage or harm by accident
	2. Build control system to avoid accidental modification of data 
3. **I**ntent
	1. Refers to the mindset of the culprit
	2. Build legal defenses
### Ethical Issues
1. Do not use computer without authorisation
2. Do not use computer to commit crimes
3. Do not use computer to spread malware 
4. Do not install pirated software. 
5. Respect others' privacy
# Privacy
Refers to individuals' rights to restrict or deny access or usage of their data
## Aspects of privacy
1. **S**ensitive data
	1. Finances
	2. Identity
	3. IC number
2. **C**ontrolled disclosure
	1. You have some form of control but once your information is given to others, you no longer have control over how it is used
3. **A**ffected parties
	1. Organisations
	2. Companies
	3. Individuals
## Dimensions of privacy
**Information**
1. **C**ollection
	1. Only specified data is collected with consent and knowledge
2. **U**sage
	1. Data collected is only used for specified purpose
3. **R**etention
	1. Data is only retained for a certain period of time
4. **S**ecurity
	1. Necessary mechanism and precautions are taken to protect users' data
5. *E*
6. **D**isclosure
	1. Only authorised people are allowed to view the data collected
## Consent & Control of Data and Information
### Consent
1. We are information of the data collected and what it is for explicitly
2. Usually informed through Terms of use
### Control
1. In some countries such as European Union, The owner of the data is the people themselves and they need to provide permission on how it is used
2. In other countries such as America, the owner of the data is the holder of the data
	1. Which has a whole host of issues
