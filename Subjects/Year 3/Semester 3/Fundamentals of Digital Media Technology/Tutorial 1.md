# Question III

1. More captivating
	- There is more engagement from the students to the material.
	- Incorporating interactable audio, video, occasional graphic and animation can break the monotony from just reading texts.
2. More effective learning
	- Engaging as many senses as possible has been known to be more effective when retaining information when studying.
	- Incorporating multimedia may cultivate active and interactive engagement to the material.
	- Students can be part of the class rather than just being an audience passively participating in the lecture
3. Easier to convey information 
	- Educators can use interactivity tool such as drawing and highlighting to emphasise information or to simplify esoteric concepts.
	- For example, complex physics concepts can be simplified drastically when the educator is able to draw visualisations freely.
