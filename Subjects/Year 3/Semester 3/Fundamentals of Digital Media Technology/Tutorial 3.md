# Question 1 
*In what ways does a storyboard support and guide a multimedia production team*

1. Provide an overview of the project
	- This allows everyone on the same team to be on the same page.
	- The working storyboard used amongst team member contains the necessary technical information to realise the project, allowing each member to understand the work that needs to be done and the time needed.
2. Illustrate the links among screens
	- Provides a visual plan for the team
	- The team is able to understand the flow of one scene to another in a form that is easier to understand, as opposed to just texts
3. Organises workflow
	- Through storyboard, the team is able to understand and prioritise the work necessary to turn the slides in the storyboard into reality. 
	- Storyboards generally break the project into smaller, manageable parts, allowing the team to divide the work amongst themselves, estimate time, and track progress.
4. Improve collaboration
5. Identifies issues early    
6. Support client communication

1. Hardware and software requirements
	- It is necessary to determine the requirements of the software that the machine has to fulfil.
	- This is important because it is often cheaper to replace the software rather than replacing multiple machines. 
	- For example,
		- Is the authoring tool executable on a MacOS machine
		- Is the authoring tool light enough in terms of memory and graphics demand that multiple machines can run the software smoothly
2. The cost of the authoring tool
	- Is the authoring tool free to use?
	- If the authoring tool is not free to use, 
		- Is the license a one-time purchase or subscription based
3. Features included in the authoring tool
	- Certain authoring tools are made to be used by beginners
		- These authoring tools often do not include features that allow the authors to express their ideas as freely and granularly
		- As a result, these authoring tools became more limiting to professionals. 
	- For example, Canva is made to be an easier alternative that can be picked up by anyone, but suffers from a lack of features that provide users with the granular control over their work that Microsoft PowerPoint have.

    - The maturity of the tool

## Question A
*Describe any three possible problems when a multimedia project is not well managed*

1. Exceed deadline
	- When a project is not well managed, it is more likely for tasks to fail in meeting the determined requirements.
	- This forces the team to delay subsequent tasks as the failed tasks would have to be redone.  
	- As the delay compound, the predetermined deadline of the project will not be met, causing clients to be upset. 
2. Exceed allocated budget
	- Whenever a task takes longer to complete, especially passed the predetermined deadline, it often exceeds the allocated resources.
	- The additional resources used are often taken from the allocated resources for other specific tasks. 
	- As the trend continue, the team may run out of the initially allocated resources, requiring the project manager to provision more resources, spending more money.
	- For example, the development of games that are ill-managed often cost more money. The causes of this issue often revolve around:
		- Last-minute requirement changes
		- Unreasonable layoffs
3. The requirements are not fulfilled
	- At the foresight of exceeding deadline and the allocated budget, project managers often cut corners, creating a project that, at first glance, meets the requirements. However, upon scrutiny, the product is often haphazard and breaks easily.
	- Poor management may lead to a lack of style guide or asset pipeline, whereby certain assets look polished while others look amateur. The final product feels disjointed and unfinished
	- The team may manage deliver a product that technically fulfils the requirement, but the product may have other non-functional requirements such as lagging

### Question B
*Illustrate any two reasons why a team of skilled individuals is required to create a good multimedia project*

1. Ensure the project can be completed on time
	- By having a skilled team, no time and money has to be spent on training, allowing the team to allocate more resources on the project itself.
	- Skilled individuals are also more efficient in their craft, possibly reducing the overall time and resources needed to complete the project.
	- Skilled individuals are also more experienced, allowing them to anticipate potential problems and resolve them rapidly
2. Reduce risk of mismatched expectations
	- Skilled individuals are not just who are efficient in their technical prowess, but also those who can communicate their ideas and thought process clearly.
	- If the team is unable to convey their ideas with each other clearly, the end product is destined to disjointed, if they manage to finish the project.
	- Communication skill is also necessary to ensure that most of the necessary information can be extracted from the clients and understood by the team member. 

# Question IV
*Illustrate any four reasons the importance of authoring tools in the development of multimedia projects*

1. Saves time and cost
	- Rather than developing the authoring tools from scratch, utilising existing out-of-the-box authoring tools allows the team to start working on the project quickly
2. Integrate multiple multimedia types
	- These tools support all five multimedia:
		1. Text
		2. Video
		3. Audio
		4. Animation

	- These tools are often presented with a Graphical User Interface, allowing the user to develop their projects in a more intuitive manner
4. Speed up production
	- The authoring tools used are often targeted at non-programmers, so most of the features developed are high-level and utilises drag-and-drop characteristics..
	- Even if the features are complex, they often do not involve any form of programming.
