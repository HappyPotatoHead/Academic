# Question 1
*Despite the ability of computers to present advanced media like video and audio, explain any four reasons why text still remains as an important element in multimedia?*

1. Clarity and Precision
2. Support Accessibility
    - Text allows the text-to-speech feature to be implemented for the visually impaired
    - It also allows those who are hard of hearing to access the content
3. Efficient for Quick Communication
4. Enhances Searchability
