# Question 2

*Both "*Morphing*" and "*Warping*" are special effects in motion pictures and animations. Briefly discuss the similarities and differences between these two techniques for creating special effects.

1. Similarities
	- both are digital visual effect
    - Visual transformation
        - Transforming image or objects to make visually engaging effects.
    - Used in Animation and Film
        - Enhance storytelling and add visual visual interest
        - These can be used as plot points, create an atmosphere or drive the story forward. 
    - Digital Manipulation
        - Rely on digital image processing to alter shapes, appearances, or transitions
2. Differences
    - Morphing
        - The source image is gradually distorted and vanished while producing the target image
        - At least 2 images are used.
    - Warping
        - Manipulation of an image such that any shapes portrayed in the image have been significantly distorted. 
        - Only a single image is used.

5 differences between `.gif` and `.swf`

File Size

Usually the smaller of the two given it's limitations and compression options
Usually the larger of the two given it's extra capabilities like sound and interactivity.

> gifs are larger (raster)
> swf are smaller (vector)

Audio Capability

.gif files cannot store audio data
.swf files can store audio data.

Browser Compatibilty
.gif files work with all standard browsers 
.sw files require a plug-in to be installed before it runs on any
browser.

Security
.gif files are harmless as a result of their limitations, they cant be easily exploited. 
.swf files can be made to do a lot of things, given the amount if things it's capable of, it can often be easily exploited by hackers.

Colour Options 
.gif files have a very limited colour palette. 
.sw files have a much larger colour palette.

Image Quality 
.gif files are of a poor quality because of the way they are compressed.
.swf files are usually of a good quality in comparison to .gif files

Interactivity 
.gif files can't be interacted without additional image map
.swf files have so many interactive capabilities that they are sometimes used in game development
