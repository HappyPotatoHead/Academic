# Question 1

Multimedia transforms a static digital storefront into an engaging, persuasive environment. Its primary roles include:

- **Product Visualization:** High-resolution images and 360-degree views allow customers to inspect details, reducing uncertainty before a purchase.
- **Demonstration:** Videos show products in action (e.g., a "how-to" for a kitchen gadget), which is often more effective than a text description.  
- **Brand Storytelling:** Audio and high-quality graphics help build an emotional connection and brand identity.
- **Customer Support:** Interactive tutorials and chatbots using multimedia can guide users through troubleshooting or assembly.

# Question 2

A **non-interactive multimedia application**, or passive media, is one where the user has no control over the flow or sequence of the content. They start the media, and it plays from beginning to end without their input.

**Examples:**
- **A Cinema Movie:** You watch the sequence of images and audio exactly as the director intended; you cannot change the plot or the ending.
- **A Digital Signage Loop:** The advertisements you see on screens in malls or airports that cycle through images and videos automatically.

# Question 3

To learn swimming styles (like Freestyle or Butterfly), interactivity can be implemented through:

1. **Branching Navigation:** A menu where the user clicks on a specific style (e.g., "Breaststroke") to view a dedicated video, rather than watching a long video of all styles.
2. **Interactive 3D Models:** A feature where the user can click and drag their mouse to rotate a 3D swimmer, viewing the specific arm movements and body rotation from different angles.

### Question 4: Interactive Multimedia in Business Activities
1. **Corporate Training Simulations:** Virtual Reality (VR) or interactive software used to train employees on safety procedures or complex machinery.
2. **Virtual Tours (Real Estate):** Interactive 360-degree walkthroughs of properties that allow potential buyers to "move" through rooms at their own pace.
3. **Kiosks (Self-Service):** Interactive touch-screens in retail banks or fast-food restaurants (like McDonald’s ordering screens) that facilitate transactions.

### Question 5: Benefits of Multimedia in Productions
- **Increased Engagement:** Multi-sensory input (sight and sound) holds the audience's attention longer than text alone.
- **Improved Retention:** People generally remember information better when it is presented through a combination of visual and auditory channels.
- **Simplification of Complex Concepts:** Simulations and animations can break down difficult processes (like a chemical reaction or a game mechanic) into digestible visuals.
- **Accessibility:** Multimedia can cater to different learning styles (visual, auditory, and kinesthetic).

### Question 6: Interactivity in Online Surveys
Interactivity enhances online surveys by:
- **Reducing "Survey Fatigue":** Interactive elements like sliders, drag-and-drop ranking, and progress bars make the experience less tedious than a list of checkboxes.
- **Skip Logic (Branching):** The survey can automatically hide irrelevant questions based on the user's previous answers, making the survey shorter and more relevant.
- **Real-time Feedback:** Providing instant visual confirmation (like a "thank you" animation or a summary of results) encourages the user to complete the task.
