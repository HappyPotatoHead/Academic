# Cybersecurity Act

This act is more concerned with protecting the nation's cybersecurity rather than punishing criminals that had committed cybercrimes. Its sections revolve around ensuring that companies comply and implemented cybersecurity systems that are secure.

This act also mainly effects:
1. National Critical Information Infrastructure
	- Any computers or systems that when compromised will negatively impact government activities and functions, public safety, and public order
2. These NCII have to:
	- Conduct cybersecurity risk assessment
	- Auditing
	- Reporting any cybersecurity incident
	- Provision of information when requested
	- Comply to code of practice
3. Cybersecurity service providers
	- They have to have a license.

# Personal Data Protection Act

This act is necessary to ensure that companies are responsible with handling user data. They're necessary to ensure that user data is not exploited for maximum profit, ignoring user's privacy rights.

According to this act:
1. Companies should be transparent with how they use user data
2. Companies should be transparent about the data that they collect
3. Companies should not sell user data to third parties without notifying the user
4. Users are given the rights to request the companies to remove their data
5. Users are given the rights to transfer their data to another location
6. Companies should ensure that their security is strong to prevent data breaches

# Communication and Multimedia Act

1. Section 211
	- No content applications service provider, or other person using a content application service, shall shall provide content which is indecent, obscene, false, menacing, or offensive in character with intent to annoy, abuse, threaten, or harass any person.
2. Section 233
	- The misuse of network facilities, tools, and technology to make, create, or facilitate, or to disseminate materials that are offensive, obscene, or inappropriate with the intention to annoy, harass, or threaten someone

Punishment is a fine not exceeding RM50,000, or an imprisonment of not exceeding 1 year, or both, or an additional fine of Rm1,000 for each day in which the offense is continued after conviction.

# MBOT's Code of Ethics

1. Benevolence
	- Professionals should use their IT skills to help or improve the lives of others
	- They should not use them for malicious intentions
	- Building a stronger cybersecurity system
2. Integrity
	- This refers to the wholeness of character
	- The person should be honest and not commit cyber related crimes such as piracy
3. Professionalism
	- A professional should treat everyone equally regardless of who they are
4. Strive to Perfection
	- A professional should be looking to improve their skills whenever possible
	- Attend talks, workshops, or tutorials
	- Lean new technologies and skills to keep up with the changing time
5. Diligence
	- A professional should complete their work thoroughly and within due time. 
	- They should not complete their tasks haphazardly
	- They should at least meet the customer's expectation
6. Competence
	- Professionals should not falsify their credentials or anything that accredit their competence
		- They should not falsify their degrees or certificates.
	- Professionals should also be good at what they claim they can do
		- If they find themselves lacking with new skills, they should search ways to learn or improve such skills

# Hacking Hat Colours

1. White hat
	- Hacker who assist in testing the safety of systems
	- They hack legally
2. Black hat
	- Hackers who hack with malicious intentions, often for personal financial gains
3. Red hat
	- Hackers who target black hat hackers 
	- They have noble intentions but are operating illegally
4. Gray hat hackers
	- In between white hat and black hat hackers. 
	- They still use illegal means to hack into systems, but they often reveal what they found
5. Blue hat
	- Hackers who are hacking for revenge 
	- Hackers who are employed by companies to test their systems
